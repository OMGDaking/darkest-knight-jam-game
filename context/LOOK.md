# Look

How the game looks and sounds. References help Claude more than adjectives.

## Mood

*Three or four words for how it should feel.*

Example: "Foggy, quiet, lonely, warm light."

> Creepy, cursed, quiet — then sudden violence. Warm gold light inside a cold, dark castle.

## Palette

*Colors, or a description of them.*

Example: "Grey-blue sea, near-black sky, one warm yellow for the beam."

> Near-black and charcoal for the world; cold steel blue-grey for armour; ember orange and gold for lanterns, sparks and the castle hall; blood red for damage.
