# Mechanic

How the game plays. Jam rule: the whole game is built around **one action the player repeats** (jump, dodge, run, aim, time a tap). Think Subway Surfers, Flappy Bird, or the Chrome dino.

## The one action

*What is the single action the player repeats, and what makes doing it again feel good?*

Example: "Sweep the beam, spot a ship, hold the light on it until it reaches the harbor."

> Parry. The enemy attacks every 2.5 to 3 seconds, and you press at the right moment to turn the blade away. A good parry drains their gauge. When the gauge is empty they're open, and you attack them until they're defeated.

## 500 times

*Would you do this action 500 times? What's different each time?*

Example: "Yes: the ships come at different speeds and angles, so every sweep is a new timing."

> Yes — every attack is a new read. The enemy swings every 2.5 to 3 seconds and picks light or heavy at random, so no two parries are the same timing.

## Controls

*Which keys or mouse actions, and what does each do?*

Example: "Mouse moves the beam. Click to flash it."

> - Right mouse — parry (the one action)
> - Left mouse — attack, only useful when the enemy's gauge is empty
> - Left / right — dodge, for heavy attacks (deferred, see Scope)
> - Enter — start and restart

## Win and lose

*How does a round end? What counts as doing well?*

Example: "Lose when three ships hit the rocks. Score is ships saved."

> A fight is won when the enemy's gauge is empty and you've attacked them until they're defeated. You lose when your health runs out. Health does not regenerate during a fight, but goes back to full when you win one. Three fights in a row. **[OPEN — what earns score?]**
